# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: March 2, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled on https://apps-h3p.com.

## 1. Data collection

This documentation site is designed to minimize data collection.

- No user account is required.
- No first-party advertising trackers are intentionally installed.
- No payment data is processed directly on this site.

For h3p apps listed on this site (including Metrics Data, Neon Vision Editor, Release Assistant, X-Newsbook, and Lingua Latina), data handling may vary by app features and platform integration. Where app-specific behavior exists, it is documented in the relevant app documentation and in linked third-party provider policies.

Lingua Latina has an app-specific policy at [Privacy Policy for Lingua Latina](/apps/lingua-latina/privacy-policy).

## 2. Technical logs

Hosting providers and CDNs may process technical request data (for example IP address, user agent, timestamps, requested URL) for security, delivery, and reliability.

## 3. Cookies and local storage

The site uses limited browser storage and technical cookies for core operation, plus optional preference storage for UI settings.
For EU/EEA users, non-essential cookie categories are not intended to be set before consent where legally required.

See [EU Cookie Notice](/policies/eu-cookie-notice).

## 4. Third-party services

Outbound links to services such as GitHub, Apple App Store, TestFlight, Patreon, and social platforms are governed by those services' own privacy policies.

Some h3p apps may integrate with third-party services chosen by the user (for example analytics providers, source-code hosting, or distribution platforms). In those cases, third-party privacy terms apply to data processed by those integrations.

## 5. Data retention

Where technical logs are processed by infrastructure providers, retention is controlled by those providers and their security policies.

## 6. Your rights (EU/EEA)

If applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability rights under GDPR, subject to legal limitations.

## 7. Contact

For privacy requests, use [Support and Feedback](/support/support-and-feedback).

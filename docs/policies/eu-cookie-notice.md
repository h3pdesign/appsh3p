# EU Cookie Notice

_Last updated: March 2, 2026_

This policy explains how https://apps-h3p.com uses cookies and similar technologies for users in the EU/EEA.
It is written to align with EU ePrivacy rules (cookie consent) and GDPR transparency requirements.

## 1. Scope and definitions

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device.
We also use "similar technologies" such as browser local storage where needed for site settings.

This policy applies to first-party pages under `apps-h3p.com`.
Third-party websites linked from this site are covered by their own policies.

## 2. Categories we use

### 2.1 Strictly necessary cookies/storage (always active)

These are required to provide the service you explicitly request (for example navigation/session behavior and technical security).
They are not used for advertising profiling.

Examples can include:

- core navigation/session state
- security and abuse-prevention state
- UI stability state needed for requested features

### 2.2 Preference storage (active when used by site features)

Preference storage remembers choices such as interface settings (for example visual effects toggle or compact mode).
Where required by law, preference storage that is not strictly necessary is only used after consent.

### 2.3 Analytics and marketing

At this time, no first-party behavioral advertising cookies are intentionally enabled on this documentation site.
If analytics is enabled in the future, it will be disclosed here with purpose, provider, retention, and consent status.

## 3. Consent model (EU/EEA)

- We do not set non-essential cookies before consent.
- Consent choices are granular by category where applicable.
- You can refuse non-essential categories and continue using the core site.
- You can withdraw or change consent at any time.

## 4. Third-party services and external links

When you open external services (for example GitHub, Apple, Patreon, X/Twitter, Instagram, Behance, 500px), those services may set their own cookies.
Those cookies are governed by the third party, not by this policy.

## 5. Retention

- Strictly necessary/session data: retained only as long as required for technical operation.
- Preference/consent state: retained only as long as needed to remember your choices or meet legal obligations.
- If analytics is added later, retention periods will be listed explicitly.

## 6. Your rights (EU/EEA)

Under GDPR, where applicable, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability.
You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

## 7. How to manage cookies

You can manage cookies via:

- this site’s consent controls (where shown)
- your browser settings (block/delete cookies and local storage)

Blocking strictly necessary storage can break core functionality.

## 8. Contact

For privacy or cookie requests, use [Support and Feedback](/support/support-and-feedback).

## 9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when technology, legal requirements, or site features change.
Material changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

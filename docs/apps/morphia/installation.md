# Morphia Installation

## App Store

Download [Morphia: Living Art on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/morphia-living-art/id6833573410). It is free, with an optional one-time Studio purchase and no subscription. The Apple Watch app is included and installs from the Watch app on your iPhone.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 18 or later
- for the Watch app: Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later
- a Metal-capable device (all devices that run iOS 18 qualify)

## First Run

1. Open Morphia. The Coats canvas is ready to draw on immediately.
2. Drag a finger across the canvas to seed a pattern.
3. Use the mode picker at the top left to switch between Coats, Murmuration, Cephalopod Skin, Slime Mould, Fireflies, and Sunflower.
4. Open **Presets**, **Palette**, or the sliders button to tune the simulation.
5. Tap **?** for the "Why?" card that explains the biology.

## Permissions

Morphia asks for **add-only** access to Photos the first time you save an artwork or video to your photo library. It cannot read your existing photos. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

## Unlocking Studio

Tap any locked preset, palette, Mutate, or the time-lapse button to see the Studio offer. **Restore purchase** on the same screen restores a previous purchase made with the same Apple Account.

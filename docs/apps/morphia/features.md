# Morphia Features

- six living systems: Coats (reaction-diffusion), Murmuration (flocking), Cephalopod Skin (chromatophores), Slime Mould (Physarum networks), Fireflies (flash synchrony), and Sunflower (phyllotaxis)
- ten game modes with five levels each, 1 to 3 stars per level, endless Expeditions, and a Daily Specimen
- an "Aim for" card with the next goal, its progress, and a live meter
- deep dives and quizzes with sources from peer-reviewed research
- optional Game Center achievements and leaderboards
- Apple Watch app with the living systems on your wrist
- English and German
- touch, drag, and press-and-hold interactions specific to each mode
- presets for each mode, such as Stripes, Spots, Rosettes, Coral, Starlings, Fish school, Calm, Camouflage, and Alarm flash
- parameter sliders linked to plain-language explanations
- organism palettes: Poison Dart Frog, Nudibranch, Aurora, Peacock, Cuttlefish, and Autumn Leaf-cutter, plus Plasmodium, Firefly Night, and Sunflower, free with their mode
- "Why?" cards with one real biology fact and one thing to try
- undo, clear, pause, and resume
- local gallery of saved works, reproducible from their parameters and seed
- PNG export and time-lapse video export
- Mutate: one tap nudges every parameter for happy accidents (Studio)

## Comfort and Accessibility

- follows the system **Reduce Motion** setting, which slows the simulation and softens effects
- a separate **Reduce flashing** setting caps flash intensity in presets such as Alarm flash
- VoiceOver labels on all controls outside the canvas

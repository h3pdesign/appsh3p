# Morphia FAQ

## Does Morphia require an account?

No. There is no account, sign-in, or cloud sync.

## Does the app work offline?

Yes. Morphia runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests of its own. Only the App Store purchase and restore flow uses Apple's services.

## What does Morphia Studio cost?

Studio is a single, non-consumable in-app purchase of 9,99 € (or the local App Store equivalent). It is not a subscription.

## How do I restore my Studio purchase?

Open the Studio offer (tap any locked feature) and tap **Restore purchase**. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

## How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Where are my works stored?

Saved works and exports are stored in the app on your device. They are included in your device backup, and deleting the app deletes them. Anything you save to Photos stays in your photo library.

## Is the biology accurate?

Each "Why?" card is written in plain language and reviewed for accuracy. The simulations are simplified models of real mechanisms: Turing-style reaction-diffusion, boids flocking, and chromatophore signalling.

## How do I get support?

Use [Support and Feedback](/support/support-and-feedback) and include "Morphia" with your app version and device in the title.

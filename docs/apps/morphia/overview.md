## Application Purpose

Morphia (listed on the App Store as **Morphia: Living Art**) is a generative art studio for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch where every canvas is a real biology simulation: animal coats, starling murmurations, cuttlefish skin, slime mould, fireflies, and sunflowers. You nudge the living system and it grows the art. Short "Why?" cards, deep dives, and quizzes explain the science, with every fact sourced from peer-reviewed research.

Primary user outcomes:

- make living, touch-driven patterns within seconds of opening the app
- tune each simulation with sliders, presets, and organism-inspired palettes
- learn the real biology behind each canvas in plain language
- save works to a local gallery and export them as images or time-lapse videos
- play ten game modes with stars, Expeditions, goals, and Game Center leaderboards

## Modes

- **Coats:** Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion. Draw a chemical into the canvas and watch stripes, spots, and rosettes grow, the same idea Alan Turing proposed for animal coats.
- **Murmuration:** GPU flocking with separation, alignment, and cohesion. Draw a path for the flock to follow, or press and hold to act as a predator.
- **Cephalopod Skin:** a grid of chromatophores in yellow, red, and brown pigment layers. Tap to send a ripple, drag to paint, and hold for a warning flash.
- **Slime Mould:** 150,000 virtual Physarum cells lay and follow trails. Place oat flakes and watch the network link them, scatter salt the mould avoids, or drag to steer it.
- **Fireflies:** hundreds of fireflies nudge each other's clocks until the meadow flashes as one. Flash a lantern, hold to be a pacemaker, or drag to blow wind.
- **Sunflower:** phyllotaxis. Tune the turn angle by thousandths of a degree and see why only the golden angle (137.5°) packs the seeds perfectly.

## Games and Goals

- ten game modes with five levels each, such as Icon Labyrinth, Coat Spotter, Metamorphosis, Flock Shepherd, Flash Reflex, and Ripple Count, plus a Daily Specimen
- 1 to 3 stars per level, with the star targets shown before you start, and endless Expeditions after level 3
- an "Aim for" card on the canvas that shows the next goal, its progress, and a live meter
- quizzes and deep dives for every living system
- optional Game Center achievements and leaderboards; every game works offline without signing in

## Pricing

Morphia is free to download. Every living system, drawing, every slider, every "Why?" card, and every game mode are free.

A one-time **Morphia Studio** in-app purchase (9,99 €, or the local App Store equivalent) unlocks every preset, all six organism palettes, time-lapse video, Mutate, and an unlimited gallery. There is no subscription.

## Platform Availability

- iOS 18 or later
- iPadOS 18 or later
- watchOS 11 or later (Apple Watch app included)

Morphia is designed iPhone-first and available in English and German. Get it on the App Store as [Morphia: Living Art](https://apps.apple.com/app/morphia-living-art/id6833573410).

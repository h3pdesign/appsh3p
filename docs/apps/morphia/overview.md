## Application Purpose

Morphia is a generative art studio for iPhone and iPad where every canvas is a small living system. Real biology models (pattern formation, flocking, and cephalopod skin) drive touch-based art tools, and a short "Why?" card explains the science behind each one.

Primary user outcomes:

- make living, touch-driven patterns within seconds of opening the app
- tune each simulation with sliders, presets, and organism-inspired palettes
- learn the real biology behind each canvas in plain language
- save works to a local gallery and export them as images or time-lapse videos

## Modes

- **Coats:** Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion. Draw a chemical into the canvas and watch stripes, spots, and rosettes grow, the same idea Alan Turing proposed for animal coats.
- **Murmuration:** GPU flocking with separation, alignment, and cohesion. Draw a path for the flock to follow, or press and hold to act as a predator.
- **Cephalopod Skin:** a grid of chromatophores in yellow, red, and brown pigment layers. Tap to send a ripple, drag to paint, and hold for a warning flash.

## Pricing

Morphia is free to download. All three modes, drawing, every slider, and every "Why?" card are free.

A one-time **Morphia Studio** in-app purchase (9,99 €, or the local App Store equivalent) unlocks every preset, all six organism palettes, time-lapse video, Mutate, and an unlimited gallery. There is no subscription.

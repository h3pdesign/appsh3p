# Morphia Changelog

## 1.2.0 (November 14, 2026)

- Now on the App Store, with three new living systems: Slime Mould, Fireflies, and Sunflower, each with presets, games, a quiz, and an Apple Watch version.
- A new "Aim for" card shows the next goal, its progress, and a live meter, and every game level shows what each star needs.
- Also includes the 1.1.0 work, which was not released on its own: five new game modes, 1 to 3 stars per level, endless Expeditions, and new Game Center achievements and leaderboards.
- [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/morphia-living-art/id6833573410)

## Renamed from ArtiBol (November 11, 2026)

- The app formerly documented as ArtiBol is now Morphia. Old `/apps/artibol/` links redirect to the new pages.

## 0.1.0 (in development, November 2026)

- First project build with Coats, Murmuration, and Cephalopod Skin modes, "Why?" cards, organism palettes, gallery, PNG and time-lapse export, and the one-time Studio unlock.
- App documentation entry and privacy policy added to apps-h3p.com.
- Not yet published on the App Store.

# Liquid Record Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Liquid Record is an offline-first audio recorder. H3P does not operate an account service for Liquid Record and does not collect, sell, track, or use personal data for advertising.

## Microphone Access

Microphone permission is requested when you first choose to record. Speech Recognition permission is requested when the transcription technology on your device requires it. Recording pauses when the app becomes inactive; Liquid Record does not record in the background.

To change or revoke access:

- Open **Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > Liquid Record** to turn microphone access off. Recording then becomes unavailable until you allow access again.
- Where Speech Recognition permission has been requested, open **Settings > Privacy & Security > Speech Recognition > Liquid Record** to revoke it. Features that require that permission will no longer work until access is restored.
- Background playback is optional and off by default. Turn it off inside Liquid Record under **System > Audio > Playback**. When enabled, the recording title and playback state are supplied to Apple's system playback controls, including the Lock Screen and Control Center.
- Stop importing or refreshing a linked podcast RSS feed to stop requests to its provider. Liquid Record does not sign you into Spotify or hold Spotify credentials.

Revoking a permission or disabling an optional feature does not automatically delete recordings, transcripts, or exported copies. The deletion options are described below. System Settings labels may vary by operating-system version.

## Local Data

- Recordings are saved locally as user-created audio files.
- Recording metadata, markers, notes, cover artwork, waveform cache data, and app settings remain local to the device.
- Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech technologies. On supported devices, optional summaries use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Audio and transcript content are not sent to H3P.
- Export may create a separate audio or text copy in the app's **Documents / Liquid Record / Exports** folder before sharing. These copies are not guaranteed to disappear when you delete the original recording; remove them separately when no longer needed.

## Optional Network Access

Liquid Record does not require an H3P account. The app does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. Its network-backed features are:

- StoreKit communicates with Apple when the app prepares its product price and entitlement state, and when you purchase or restore the one-time Full Access purchase. Apple processes those transactions under its own privacy policy.
- If you enter a public podcast RSS feed, Liquid Record downloads that feed directly to display its public show and episode metadata. The request is made only when you choose to link or refresh the feed.
- Opening Spotify for Creators, support, privacy, or legal pages hands the destination to the system browser. Liquid Record does not request or store Spotify credentials.

Liquid Record does not upload recordings, transcripts, notes, or project files to H3P servers.

An RSS request discloses the requested feed URL and ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to the feed host and its delivery providers. Those providers may retain request logs under their own policies. Only use a feed whose provider you trust. Importing episode metadata does not upload your recordings. Optional speech and Apple Intelligence models may need to be downloaded from Apple before on-device processing is available.

## Files and Sharing

Files selected through Apple's document picker are accessed for the operation you request. Imported audio is copied into the app's local project storage. If you assign an external Apple Files folder, the app keeps a local folder reference so you can request future exports to that folder. Assignment does not upload recordings automatically or move the originals out of the app.

Exports to Apple Files, an assigned folder, the system share sheet, or another app create separate copies managed by the destination and its provider. A destination may be on your device, in iCloud Drive, or with another provider you choose. Its privacy policy and retention settings apply to the copy it receives.

## Purchases

Full Access is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase, not a subscription. The app verifies Apple's transactions locally to determine access. H3P does not receive your payment-card details and does not operate a purchase-account server for Liquid Record.

## Retention, Deletion, and Backups

Recordings and related project information are kept in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app. Open **Library / Projects**, use the recording's **More (...) > Delete** action, and confirm deletion to remove its local audio and linked markers, notes, and transcript. Removing a recording does not remove podcast cover artwork or separately exported copies. Project preferences, podcast links, cover artwork, and the record that the free recording allowance was used are managed separately; deleting a recording does not reset that allowance or cancel a purchase.

Deleting the app removes its local container, including recordings and app-created export copies still stored there. Data may also be included in device or iCloud backups according to your Apple settings. Removing the app does not delete existing backups, external-folder files, or copies you previously exported, shared, or published. Delete those through the relevant destination, provider, or Apple backup settings. Liquid Record cannot recall files already shared with another person or service. Apple's purchase history is managed by Apple, not by deleting this app.

H3P has no server copy of your recordings to retrieve or delete. Do not submit private recordings, transcripts, credentials, or sensitive personal details in public support issues. Contact H3P through the support routes below for privacy questions; do not send sensitive content without first agreeing on an appropriate private channel.

# Liquid Record Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Liquid Record is an offline-first audio recorder. H3P does not operate an account service for Liquid Record and does not collect, sell, track, or use personal data for advertising.

## Microphone Access

Microphone permission is requested only from the first record action. If permission is denied, the console remains accessible and should provide a path to Settings.

## Local Data

- Recordings are saved locally as user-created audio files.
- Recording metadata, markers, notes, waveform cache data, and app settings remain local to the device.
- Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech technologies. On supported devices, optional summaries use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Audio and transcript content are not sent to H3P.
- Export uses temporary staging before handing the file to the system share flow.

## Optional Network Access

Liquid Record does not require an H3P account. Network access occurs only when you choose a network-backed feature:

- StoreKit communicates with Apple to load, purchase, and restore the one-time Full Access in-app purchase. Apple processes those transactions under its own privacy policy.
- If you enter a public podcast RSS feed, Liquid Record downloads that feed directly to display its public show and episode metadata. The request is made only when you choose to link or refresh the feed.
- Opening Spotify for Creators, support, privacy, or legal pages hands the destination to the system browser. Liquid Record does not request or store Spotify credentials.

Liquid Record does not upload recordings, transcripts, notes, or project files to H3P servers.

## Files and Sharing

Files selected through Apple's document picker are accessed only for the operation you request. Exported files are shared only with the destination you select in the system share sheet.

## Purchases

Full Access is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Apple processes purchase and restoration data; H3P receives only the entitlement state needed to unlock features.

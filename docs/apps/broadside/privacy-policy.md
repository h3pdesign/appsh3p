# Privacy Policy for Broadside

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

Broadside is a newspaper-style reader for RSS and Atom feeds, Feedly and X. The app does not require an account and does not include ads, tracking, or developer telemetry. The developer does not collect data from this app.

## Data Stored Locally

The app stores your feed subscriptions, reading and saved state, display settings (theme, fonts, text size, pictures), and the most recent edition locally on the device. This data is used only for app functionality and is not transmitted to developer-controlled servers.

## Feeds and Articles

Feeds you subscribe to are fetched directly from your device from the addresses you add. Pictures in those feeds are loaded from the publishers' servers. When you choose "Print the full story here", the app loads that article's page from the publisher once to display its text. These requests go straight to the publishers; the developer does not operate a server in between.

## Optional Sources: Feedly and X

Feedly and X are optional. If you connect them, the app sends requests only to Feedly (cloud.feedly.com) or X (api.x.com) using the access token or sign-in you provide. Tokens and the X Client ID are stored in the system Keychain on your device. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

## OPML Import and Export

OPML files are read or written only when you pick a file. Their contents stay on your device.

## Purchases

Broadside Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details. The app checks your subscription status locally through StoreKit.

## No Ads or Tracking

The app does not use tracking technologies and does not share data with third parties for advertising or tracking. There is no developer analytics and no background behavioural measurement.

## App Store Privacy Details

- Data is not collected.
- No data is used for tracking.
- No data is linked to the user's identity.

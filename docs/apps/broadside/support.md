# Broadside Support

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

Need help with Broadside? Start here, then reach out through [Support and Feedback](/support/support-and-feedback) if something still doesn't work.

## Getting Started

- On first launch, tap **Add starter feeds** or open **Sources** and paste any feed or website address. Broadside finds the site's feed automatically.
- Import your existing subscriptions with **Sources → Import OPML…**, and export them with **Export OPML…**.
- Pull to refresh, or press ⌘R on Mac. Editions older than 30 minutes refresh when you return to the app.

## Common Questions

**A feed won't add.** Check that the address opens in a browser. Some sites only offer a feed on a specific URL (often ending in `/rss`, `/feed` or `.xml`).

**"Already in your paper."** That feed is already subscribed, even if the address differs by `www.`, `http` or a trailing slash.

**Feedly or X don't load.** Both need your own credentials: a Feedly developer access token, or an X developer app Client ID with the callback `xnewsbook://oauth-callback` and an X API plan that includes the home timeline.

**Pictures look dotted.** That's the halftone setting. Switch to Black & White or Full colour in **Settings → Pictures**.

## Broadside Plus

The free edition carries up to 5 feeds, the Broadsheet theme, and halftone or black & white pictures. Broadside Plus adds unlimited feeds, Feedly and X, all five themes, full-colour pictures, full-story printing and font choices.

- Subscribe, restore purchases or manage your subscription in **Settings → Broadside Plus**.
- Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

# Privacy Policy for ArtiBol

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

ArtiBol is a generative art app. It does not require an account and does not include ads, tracking, analytics, or developer telemetry. The developer does not collect data from this app.

## Data Stored Locally

ArtiBol stores your settings, saved works (parameters, seed, palette, and thumbnail), and exported images and videos inside the app on your device. This data is used only for app functionality and is never transmitted to developer-controlled servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Photos

If you choose to save an artwork or time-lapse video to your photo library, ArtiBol asks for add-only Photos access. The app can add items to your library but cannot read, browse, or upload your existing photos.

## Network

ArtiBol makes no network requests of its own. It contains no third-party SDKs.

## In-App Purchase

The optional ArtiBol Studio unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status locally through Apple's StoreKit.

## No Ads or Tracking

The app does not use tracking technologies and does not share data with third parties for advertising or tracking.

## Children

ArtiBol does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## App Store Privacy Details

- Data is not collected.
- No data is used for tracking.
- No data is linked to the user's identity.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

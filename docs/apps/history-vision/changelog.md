# History Vision Changelog

## October 2026 private-beta project updates (build 262)

- Expanded and revised source-backed entries across ancient, medieval, and modern history, including new material on statue-menhir traditions and corrections to chronology, attribution, and regional assignments.
- Merged duplicate Los Millares coverage and refined editorial records through 25 content-expansion rounds.
- Refreshed the bundled database. Build 262 currently contains 2,079 articles, 6,635 timeline records, 4,532 research sources, and 4,868 structured historical claims.
- This is private-beta project work, not a public release; article/source record counts are catalogue metadata, not claims of independent verification.

## 1.0 beta - September 2026 baseline

- Expanded the complete searchable library to 295 bundled articles.
- Added source-backed editorial reading, inline references, source inspection, related people and inventions, private notes, and review state.
- Introduced Discover recommendations, Continue Reading, period and region browsing, and optional daily features.
- Added research workflows with clearly labelled optional Apple Foundation Models assistance.
- Reworked chronology around 13,387 dated records with list, comparison, filtering, uncertainty semantics, and a separate archived-reference layer.
- Added an offline Apple Watch companion for briefs, chronology, sources, and reading-list handoff.
- Introduced the archway-and-sun app icon across supported Apple platforms.

The initial September documentation snapshot recorded iOS build **225**, macOS build **115**, and watchOS build **115**; the October project snapshot is build **262** as listed above.

## 1.0 - April 2026

- Published the initial private-beta reader, source model, timeline blocks, and app documentation.

# Privacy Policy for History Vision

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

History Vision is a history reader for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. The app does not require an account and does not include ads, tracking, or developer telemetry. The developer does not collect data from this app.

## Data Stored Locally

The app stores settings, saved articles, article notes, reading positions, recently opened articles, recent searches, and research collections (questions, notes, source references and evidence reviews) locally on the device. This data is used only for app functionality and is not transmitted to developer-controlled servers.

## Offline Catalogue

The article catalogue is a read-only database inside the app bundle. It does not contain user data.

## Optional Online References

Online lookups are optional and only run when you start them, for example a reference search, suggested references or the daily featured topic. For these requests the app contacts Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons, operated by the Wikimedia Foundation. The request contains the search term or article title and the standard technical data of an internet connection, such as your IP address. The Wikimedia Foundation's [Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy) applies to these requests.

When you open a cited source or a Wikipedia link, it opens in your browser and that website's privacy policy applies.

## Apple Intelligence

When Apple Intelligence is available, the app can use Apple's on-device system model for optional briefings. Processing is handled by Apple's system functionality. The developer does not operate a server for this feature and does not receive these requests.

## Apple Watch

The iPhone app and the Apple Watch app keep your list of saved articles in sync through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This transfer stays between your own paired devices. The Watch app includes its own offline catalogue.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with StoreKit on the device.

## No Ads or Tracking

The app does not use tracking technologies and does not share data with third parties for advertising or tracking. There is no developer analytics and no background behavioral measurement.

## App Store Privacy Details

- Data is not collected.
- No data is used for tracking.
- No data is linked to the user's identity.

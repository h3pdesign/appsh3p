# History Vision Features

## Discover

- Rotating featured reads and period-balanced recommendations.
- Continue Reading, recent articles, and saved-article access.
- Browse by historical period or world region.
- Optional online daily feature with a clear local-content fallback.

## Complete Library

- Search all 295 bundled articles by title, period, and region.
- Compact, lazy-loading catalogue rows with stable article identity.
- Filters for saved articles and source-quality state.
- Featured, recent, and suggested article collections.

## Article Reader

- Typography-first long-form reading with section navigation and text sizing.
- Key facts, historical images, article keywords, related articles, people, and inventions.
- Reading progress, bookmarks, reviewed state, and private article notes.
- Inline reference markers, source inspection, licensing details, citation-list copying, and sharing with sources.

## Research And AI

- Search across the local catalogue and prepare source-aware research articles.
- Optional on-device Apple Foundation Models assistance where the platform supports it.
- Explicit labels distinguish retrieved evidence, model-generated text, Wikipedia archive material, and content still needing review.

## Timeline And Comparison

- Search 13,387 dated records with year, region, period, and text filters.
- Separate curated events and intervals from unreviewed archived date mentions.
- List and comparison presentations with shared chronology, semantic types, uncertainty labels, and source links.
- Dense-timeline navigation designed for progressive filtering instead of rendering every record as an equal card.

## Apple Watch Companion

- Offline daily brief, reading list, and curated chronology.
- Source links and cited excerpts remain available from each brief.
- A bounded saved-article snapshot syncs from iPhone through WatchConnectivity.
- Save-on-iPhone requests queue safely when the phone is temporarily unreachable.

## Personalisation And Accessibility

- Multiple reading backgrounds, article-card density, and source-label preferences.
- Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, keyboard navigation on Mac, and non-colour timeline symbols.
- Local bookmarks, recent-reading state, review state, and notes.

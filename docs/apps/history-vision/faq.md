# History Vision FAQ

## Is History Vision focused on one region or period?

No. The 295-article catalogue spans periods and regions and can be browsed or filtered through both dimensions.

## Will all pages include citations?

Source-backed editorial paragraphs include inline reference markers and source details. Archived or fallback material remains labelled separately, and a citation does not imply independent historical review.

## Does History Vision work offline?

The bundled article catalogue and the Apple Watch brief catalogue work offline. Opening external sources or loading the optional online daily feature requires a connection.

## What does the timeline contain?

The current database contains 13,387 dated records. Curated events, people, periods, and themes are kept distinct from unreviewed date mentions extracted from archived source text.

## Does History Vision use AI?

Optional research assistance can use Apple Foundation Models on supported devices. Generated text is labelled, grounded in the available source packet where possible, and is not treated as an independently verified historical source.

## Which platforms are supported?

The main app supports iPhone, iPad, and Mac. A native watchOS companion provides offline briefs, chronology, sources, and reading-list access.

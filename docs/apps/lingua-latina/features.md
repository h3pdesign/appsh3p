# Lingua Latina Features

- Latin dictionary lookup by lemma or inflected form
- morphology analysis with multiple parses for ambiguous forms
- grammar reference for declensions, conjugations, participles, infinitives, case syntax, clauses, and subjunctive usage
- vocabulary decks, saved words, review sessions, and progress tracking
- localized output in German or English based on app language
- paste-based Latin text analysis with tap-to-lookup workflows
- optional Apple Intelligence, OpenAI, or local OpenAI-compatible provider support

## Dictionary

Entries can include lemma, part of speech, morphology summary, localized meanings, examples, principal parts, and related words.

## Study

Quiz direction follows the selected app language:

- Latin to German and German to Latin
- Latin to English and English to Latin

## AI Assistance

AI output is optional and distinct from core dictionary data. AI does not override bundled lexical data.

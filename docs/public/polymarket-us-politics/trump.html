Appendix

Methodology

What Are Prediction Markets?

Prediction markets are exchange-traded markets where participants buy and sell contracts that pay out based on the outcome of future events. Prices reflect the crowd's aggregate probability assessment for each outcome. Unlike polls or pundit opinions, prediction markets are backed by real financial stakes — participants must put money behind their beliefs, which creates powerful incentives for accuracy. Academic research has consistently found that prediction markets outperform polls, expert panels, and statistical models at forecasting political events. The probabilities shown in this report represent the market-implied likelihood of each outcome as of February 25, 2026. Volume figures indicate total trading activity, serving as a proxy for market liquidity and information density. Higher volume generally corresponds to more reliable probability estimates.
